Base64 explained

Convert Base64 without sending your text anywhere

Base64 is a text encoding that represents data using letters, numbers, +, /, and =. It is commonly used in APIs, email attachments, data URLs, and configuration files. It is an encoding—not encryption—so do not use it to protect secrets.

  1. Choose a mode. Select Encode text for plain text or Decode Base64 for an encoded value.
  2. Paste your input. The converter supports UTF-8 text, including emojis and international characters.
  3. Convert and copy. Review the result, then use Copy when you need it elsewhere.

Frequently asked questions

Is this Base64 converter private?

Yes. Conversion is performed locally by your browser. The page has no server-side processing, analytics, cookies, local storage, or telemetry.

Can it encode emojis and non-English text?

Yes. Text is encoded as UTF-8 before conversion, so accented characters, non-Latin scripts, and emojis round-trip correctly.

Can Base64 hide sensitive information?

No. Base64 can be decoded by anyone and is not encryption. Avoid sharing passwords, access tokens, or other sensitive data in Base64 form.
